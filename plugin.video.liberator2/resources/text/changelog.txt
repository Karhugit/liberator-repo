[COLOR dodgerblue][B]Version 0.1.4[/B][/COLOR]
- Highlighted future-dated and unaired episodes in red italics across all episode title formats
- Selecting a future-dated episode now shows "Please wait for release date!" before scraping
- Added automatic playback verification and ordered fallback to the next source when a stream times out or fails (with BACK to cancel and RIGHT to skip)
